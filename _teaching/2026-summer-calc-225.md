Students learned about area under the curve, area between curves, Riemann sums, integrals as limits, antiderivatives, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, and volumes of solid of revolution. I prepared and presented lecture material in this 5 week summer course, as well as wrote and administered weekly quizzes, two midterms, and one final exam.
