Graduate Career
----
I am a second year graduate student in a PhD program for mathematics at Binghamton University. I am studying persistent homology in the field of topological data analysis with Cary Malkiewich, as well as working on a project with David White (Queens University). 

Undergraduate Career
----
I graduated from James Madison University in 2021 with a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Physics and a minor in astronomy. I did research with David Duncan in graph theory which resulted in a published paper in the Discrete Mathematics journal, titled "Characterizing immutable sandpiles: A first look".
