Here you can find an overview of my research experience, completed course work, and teaching experience during my time as a student. At the end of this page, there is information on my gap years between undergraduate and graduate school, during which I worked as an assistant cidermaker in a small business production facility.


Graduate Career
----
I am a second year graduate student in a PhD program for mathematics at Binghamton University. I am studying persistent homology in the field of topological data analysis with Cary Malkiewich. I am also working on a project with David White (Queens University). My completed course work includes: linear algebra, group theory, point set topology, algebraic topology, measure theory, and functional analysis. My current course work is in probability and finite difference methods of partial differential equations. I am a Graduate Teaching Assistant for Elementary Statistics under Marie Chazal, and I will be an instructor of record for Calculus 1 in the spring of 2027.



Undergraduate Career
----
I graduated from James Madison University in 2021 with a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Physics and a minor in astronomy. I conducted research in graph theory, studying models of discrete systems called "sandpiles" and how they behave over time. The project was done with David Duncan and culminated in a published paper in the Discrete Mathematics journal, titled "Characterizing immutable sandpiles: A first look". For more information, please visit my Publications page. I was a Learning Assistant for introductory physics courses, including basic mechanics and electromagnetism.

Gap Years
----
After graduating college in the spring of 2021 I worked at a small business in the Shenandoah Valley called Sage Bird Ciderworks. I started as a bartender and gradually became involved in the production team. I became a full time production assistant in 2023 and worked as the assistant cidermaker until I left in 2025 to return to my academic pursuits.
